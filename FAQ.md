# Bunnydoku FAQ

## What is Bunnydoku?

Bunnydoku is a colorful region logic puzzle for iOS and Android. Place hidden bunnies by using row, column, colored-region, and non-touching constraints. The puzzle is designed to be solved through deduction without guessing. Core puzzle play is available offline.

- [Official website](https://bunnydoku.app)
- [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/bunnydoku/id6774085996)
- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mi.bunnydoku)

The store listing name on both platforms is **Bunnydoku: Logic Puzzle**. The App Store lists Meta Innovation Limited as the seller. Google Play lists Meta Innovation as the publisher. Both listings identify ads and in-app purchases and describe offline play. Store availability and device compatibility can vary by region and device.

## What are the rules?

In Classic Mode, place exactly one bunny in every row, column, and colored region. No two bunnies may touch, including diagonally.

In Double Bunny Mode, place exactly two bunnies in every row, column, and colored region. The same non-touching rule applies.

## How do I place a bunny or mark an empty cell?

Quickly tap a cell twice to place a bunny. Tap once to add or remove an `X` note. Press and drag to mark or clear `X` notes across multiple cells. An incorrect mark created by the game's mistake handling cannot be manually cleared. See the [controls guide](https://bunnydoku.app/guides/how-to-place-bunnies-and-mark-x).

## How do I solve a difficult board?

Use the row, column, and colored-region counts together. Mark cells that cannot contain a bunny, then apply the non-touching rule to eliminate neighboring cells. Hints provide a next step when needed. The [solving tips guide](https://bunnydoku.app/guides/bunnydoku-tips) explains six deduction techniques.

## When does Daily Challenge unlock?

Daily Challenge unlocks after completing regular Level 20. It follows the device's local calendar date. After completing the challenge, choose one Bunny Hint or one Lightbulb Hint. See the [Daily Challenge guide](https://bunnydoku.app/guides/daily-challenge).

## How does the streak and 7-day reward work?

A streak counts consecutive local play days. The base gift on Day 7 grants 3 Bunny Hints and 3 Lightbulb Hints. The [streak guide](https://bunnydoku.app/guides/streak) has more detail.

## Can I play offline?

Yes. Regular puzzles and the Daily Challenge can be played offline. Making an in-app purchase requires an internet connection.

## Does the free version have no ads?

No. The free version may show ads. Bunnydoku supports paid removal of forced ads. This is one purchase option.

## Can I avoid an optional rewarded video?

Yes. Individual items can also be purchased and used instead of watching an optional rewarded video. This is a separate option from paid removal of forced ads. Available items and purchase terms are shown in the store for your region.

## Is Double Bunny a multiplayer mode?

No. It is a single-player, higher-difficulty mode with two bunnies required in every row, column, and colored region. Bunnies still cannot touch, including diagonally. Read the [Double Bunny guide](https://bunnydoku.app/guides/double-bunny-mode).
